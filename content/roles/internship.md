## About the role

You would work on [Mangrove](/mangrove) — real parts of it, with real consequences — in the same
room as the people building it. You take something from a question to working software, and then
watch somebody use it.

Mangrove is a Tauri application: Rust underneath, with a React and TypeScript front end. You would
write both ends of it.

We take one intern at a time. That is deliberate: teaching somebody properly takes attention, and
attention does not divide well.

## Why we do this

Companies are hiring fewer junior developers, and AI is doing the work they used to learn on. We
still believe a trade has to pass from one generation to the next, one person teaching another.

It is also the point of the company. Our motto is that through learning we flourish, and a company
that means it should be willing to spend money on somebody who is still learning.

## What you'll do

- Take one meaningful piece of work from a question to something people use, over three to six
  months.
- Sit in on the decisions — what we build, what we decline to build, and why.
- Say when something we are doing makes no sense. You will often be the only one who can see it,
  because you are the only one who has not got used to it yet.

## What we're looking for

- A habit of making things, whatever their size and however rough.
- Curiosity about why things work, not only how.
- Willingness to be taught, and willingness to argue.
- Three to six months you can spend in Mumbai, in person.

## Compensation & benefits

₹20,000 a month, paid monthly. What we quote is what reaches you.

Time off works the same for you as for everybody here: every paid day earns two points, ten points
buys a paid day off, and you take one without telling us why. Over three months that comes to
about thirteen days. If you need a day before you have earned it, take it anyway.

We would rather publish all of this than make you ask for it. You should be able to tell whether a
role is worth your time before you spend any of it writing to us.

## How to apply

Email us three things: a phone number, the days and times you are free for a ten minute call, and
a few sentences on what you want to learn on the job.

We read applications in the order they arrive and keep calling until we have found the person, so
we will write to everybody once the place is filled.
